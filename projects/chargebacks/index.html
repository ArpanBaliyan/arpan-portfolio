The business problem

Build the case before the deadline builds pressure.

A case register that connects deadlines, evidence, refund hand-offs and the next step in a dispute.

The situation

Disputes arrive with incomplete context. Some need a defensible response; others reveal a refund or hand-off the business should have completed already.

How I approach it

Keep case research, support requests and submitted rebuttals as separate stages. Match each dispute theme to an evidence checklist and investigate the underlying customer journey.

Prioritise the next deadline, establish whether the refund is genuinely owed, and prepare a concise evidence bundle for review.

The working method

The reasoning behind the work.

  • Log both case identifiers, report date, stated deadline and filed reason.
  • Investigate the order, delivery, return, credit and refund history.
  • Ask for processor guidance without marking the rebuttal as submitted.
  • Collect the evidence appropriate to the actual facts of the case.
  • Record submission separately and retain the response confirmation.
