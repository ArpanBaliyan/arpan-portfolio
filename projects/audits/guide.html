Purpose and ownership

A high call count can hide poor account checks, unclear commitments and weak documentation. A broad average can also hide one serious control failure.

Use the score to guide coaching, and treat a critical privacy or payment-handling failure as a separate escalation.

Procedure

  • Select a mix of random calls, escalations and high-value interactions.
  • Review the account evidence before listening to the conversation.
  • Score observable behaviour against the published criteria.
  • Record critical failures separately from the average quality score.
  • Assign a specific coaching action, due date and follow-up review.

Review and handover

Record the source reference, current status, next action, responsible person and review date. Keep unresolved dependencies visible. Close an item after its intended customer or operational outcome is confirmed.
