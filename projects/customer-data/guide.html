Customer data workbench

A data stewardship workbench for inconsistent names, missing contacts, possible duplicates and ownership of corrections.

Back to the workspace

Purpose and ownership

Inconsistent identifiers and contact records waste calling time, break account matching and send work to the wrong person.

Approve a correction with evidence. Flag a possible duplicate for review instead of merging two customer accounts automatically.

Procedure

  • Preserve identifiers as text, including leading zeroes.
  • Validate required fields and distinguish blanks from genuine zero values.
  • Normalise names and contact formats while retaining the original value.
  • Review possible duplicates using more than a similar name.
  • Log the approved correction, evidence, owner and date.

Review and handover

Record the source reference, current status, next action, responsible person and review date. Keep unresolved dependencies visible. Close an item after its intended customer or operational outcome is confirmed.
