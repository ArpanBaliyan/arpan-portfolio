Before handing the work over

  • State the reference and customer issue clearly enough that the next person can identify the record.
  • Attach or link the evidence. Separate a confirmed fact from something that still needs checking.
  • Ask for a specific decision or action. Explain which customer or operational outcome it enables.
  • Confirm the owner, due date and dependency. Keep the original customer commitment visible.
  • Close the loop with the customer or referring team after the outcome is confirmed.

Questions for a review conversation

Understand the decision

What is known? What is still uncertain? What could make the current action wrong? Who has the authority to decide?

Improve the next hand-off

What evidence was missing? Where did time pass without ownership? What check or clearer agreement would prevent a repeat?
