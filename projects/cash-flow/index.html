The business problem

See the cash consequence of the next decision.

A 13-week cash plan with clear opening cash, receipts, payments and adjustable timing assumptions.

The situation

An open balance is not cash available to spend. Payment timing, refunds and operating commitments affect the cash position week by week.

How I approach it

Build a visible cash rollforward and keep receipts, supplier payments, payroll and refunds distinct. Make the assumptions easy to challenge.

Compare a timing downside with the base plan before committing cash, and assign follow-ups to the receipts that matter most.

The working method

The reasoning behind the work.

  • Set the opening cash balance and the date of the forecast.
  • Separate expected receipts from already received cash.
  • List payroll, suppliers, refunds and other commitments explicitly.
  • Stress receipt timing and refund outflows without changing the base inputs.
  • Review forecast against actual receipts and explain significant variances.
