The business problem

Find the gap between two teamsâ€”and give it an owner.

An operational view of the order-to-refund journey, hand-off delays, service levels and exception resolution.

The situation

Each department can complete its own task while the customer still waits. The delay sits between pickup, scan, credit approval and refund.

How I approach it

Map the whole journey, assign an owner to each hand-off and measure elapsed time against an explicit service target.

Escalate the stalled hand-off with the evidence needed by the receiving team, rather than sending a general follow-up to everyone.

The working method

The reasoning behind the work.

  • Capture the customer's request with its original timestamp.
  • Agree the next team, required evidence and target hand-off time.
  • Track pickup, receipt, scan, credit and refund as separate events.
  • Escalate overdue work with the specific missing prerequisite.
  • Confirm the customer outcome before closing the ticket.
