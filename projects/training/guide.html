The team learning room

A structured learning path with practical case decisions, a 30-day ramp, coaching records and a knowledge assessment.

Back to the workspace

Purpose and ownership

A new colleague can make calls before they understand what makes an invoice collectible. Activity rises, but incorrect asks and avoidable escalations rise with it.

Release a learner to independent work after practical demonstrations and documented coaching, rather than after an arbitrary number of calls.

Procedure

  • Week 1: understand delivery status, aging, returns, credits and account history.
  • Week 2: shadow calls, practise polite terms explanations and log dispositions.
  • Week 3: handle supervised follow-ups and prepare evidence-based escalations.
  • Week 4: demonstrate independent decisions and consistent documentation.
  • Use observed examples and specific feedback for the next coaching session.

Review and handover

Record the source reference, current status, next action, responsible person and review date. Keep unresolved dependencies visible. Close an item after its intended customer or operational outcome is confirmed.
