How it was built

Everything runs on tools a growing company already has: Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail and Drive, at no added cost. Customer data never leaves the company's Google account.

Phase 3 reads the phase 1 workbook in place and never edits it, applies the credit method designed in phase 2 to every account each month, and checks its own totals before publishing: the AR export, the cleaned invoices and the sum of every account must agree to the cent.

The company and every record are synthetic. The processes are the ones used on a real in-house collections desk.
