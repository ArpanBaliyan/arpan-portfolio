A little about me

Close to the customer.
Across the process.

The foundationCustomer support & escalation handlingThe responsibilityCollections, major accounts & team supportThe directionClient ownership & business operations

My background is in customer-facing work: support, escalations, international calling and in-house collections. Over time, my role grew to include training colleagues, handling complex accounts and coordinating the work between departments.

At Benzeen Auto Parts, I’ve worked with California customers and a business operating across four yards. My work includes customer queries from accounting, refund escalations, chargebacks, follow-ups and reconciliation for clients with several locations.

I like being the person who follows the issue through. When a customer is waiting on a refund, the answer may sit with sales, dispatch, the yard or accounting. I find the missing step, bring the right people together and keep the customer informed.

I use Excel, Google Sheets and practical automation to keep that work visible. I’m also continuing my BA and developing my ability to analyse data and present useful decisions.
